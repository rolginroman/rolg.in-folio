# Fonts used by the Trencadís demo

`abril-fatface-latin-400.woff2`, `abril-fatface-latin-ext-400.woff2`

Abril Fatface by TypeTogether (Veronika Burian, José Scaglione), licensed under
the SIL Open Font License 1.1 — <https://openfontlicense.org>.

Self-hosted on purpose: the page makes no request to a font CDN. The files are
the `latin` and `latin-ext` subsets Google Fonts serves for
`https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Abril+Fatface`, downloaded once and
committed here.
